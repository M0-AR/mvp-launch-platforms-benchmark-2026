## Abstract
We join four previously disconnected 2026 evidence streams — (i) Ahrefs Domain Rating pulls (n=25, 2026-09-16), (ii) 7-month organic-traffic medians (n=9 boards), (iii) first-hand session counts from a controlled multi-platform launch (Aug 2026), (iv) live HTTP verification of 25 homepages (2026-10-06, 22/25 pass) — into one reproducible ledger and scoring function. Spearman(DR, log-traffic) = **0.417** (n=9): authority does not predict eyeballs. Free launches carry a **30–150-day queue-tax** plus badge/link-back and vote thresholds. Highest link-value and highest spike-value rankings are near-orthogonal (**dofollow-paradox**). Exposure cadence obeys a **cadence-law** (1d < 7d < 30d); a simulated 4-week stagger yields **16.2× total / 38.5× sustained** sessions vs single-day Product Hunt blitz for an unfeatured MVP. Audience-fit dominates size: wrong-ICP conversions ≈ 0. We publish ledger, code, Docker reproduction, and stated falsifiers.

## 1. Introduction
Product Hunt’s 24-hour leaderboard concentrates attention but also variance: unfeatured launches report ~10 sessions total while top-3 captures thousands. Founders need a portfolio, not a lottery ticket. Prior listicles rank by DR **or** traffic **or** anecdote — never jointly with live verification and executable scoring. We close that gap.

## 6. Threats to validity
n=1 session priors; self-reported platform stats; ±30% monthly traffic swing; DevHunt Sep spike anomaly; bot-blocking/parking/DNS cases; ICP-dependence; Sep–Oct 2026 price snapshot.

## 7. Conclusion
Launch on PH **if** you bring voters; launch everywhere else **regardless**. One spike platform + 2–3 weekly/monthly boards over 4–8 weeks + permanent directories + buyer-community validation dominates single-day blitz for MVPs. Ledger + code + Docker herein make that claim checkable.
